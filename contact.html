Important Notice Regarding Communication

Please note that submitting an inquiry, sending an email, or leaving a voicemail does not establish a therapist-client relationship. Confidentiality cannot be fully guaranteed across digital communications, so please limit details to non-sensitive information when contacting us online.

Emergency & Crisis Information

Rooted Mental Health does not provide immediate crisis intervention or emergency services. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, feel unsafe, or are in immediate danger of hurting yourself or someone else, please do not wait for a response from this website.

Please utilize one of the following resources immediately:

  • Life-Threatening Emergencies: Call 911 or go to your nearest hospital emergency room.
  • National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (available 24/7, free, and confidential).
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a crisis counselor 24/7.
  • Santa Clara County Emergency Psychiatric Services (EPS): Call (408) 885-6100 or the local 24-hour Suicide & Crisis Line at (855) 278-4204.
