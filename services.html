Services

Therapy is a collaborative space to process life’s complexities, understand systemic patterns, and cultivate lasting resilience. I offer tailored, evidence-based therapy for individuals, couples, and families navigating relational shifts, personal growth, and major life transitions.

Individual Therapy

Dedicated 1:1 support focused on self-insight, emotional regulation, and building practical coping strategies tailored to your unique lived experience.

Focus Areas: Life transitions, stress management, anxiety, caregiver strain, personal identity, and career changes.

Couples & Relational Therapy

A balanced, neutral space to help partners identify repetitive relational patterns, repair ruptures, and build stronger, healthier bonds.

Focus Areas: Communication challenges, premarital counseling, navigating major life changes, and deepening emotional connection.

Family & Systemic Therapy

Exploring the broader family ecosystem to improve communication, honor individual boundaries, and foster mutual understanding across generations.

Focus Areas: Generational dynamics, cross-cultural communication, family role shifts, and supporting children or young adults.

Culturally Responsive & Identity-Focused Care

Affirming, culturally humble therapy that honors how your background, culture, and community shape your worldview and mental health.

Focus Areas: Intersectional identity exploration, multicultural dynamics, and first-generation/immigrant family experiences.

Grief & Loss

A compassionate space to navigate bereavement, ambiguous loss, and the emotional changes that follow significant life transitions.

Focus Areas: Bereavement, ambiguous loss, anticipatory grief, and life transitions.

Workshops & Speaking Engagements

Psychoeducational workshops and speaking engagements centered on mental health, relationship patterns, and culturally responsive care.

Focus Areas: Mental health education, relationship patterns, cultural identity, and community wellness.
