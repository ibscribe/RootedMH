Welcome to Rooted Mental Health

Here, we believe that true healing and meaningful growth come from understanding the whole picture—where you've been, where you are, and where you want to go. Life's transitions, relationship dynamics, and individual stresses can leave us feeling disconnected or stuck. You'll find a grounded, compassionate space to pause, reflect, and cultivate lasting resilience.

Our Philosophy & Approach

Mental health isn't just about managing symptoms; it's about understanding the interconnected systems, relationships, and cultural contexts that shape our lived experiences.

Our clinical approach is relational, systemic, and culturally sensitive. We view you not as a collection of problems to be fixed, but as a complete individual embedded within relationships, families, and communities.

When you work with us, you can expect:

  • A Grounded, Collaborative Space: Therapy is a partnership. We work together to help you gain clarity, develop insight, and build practical tools tailored to your unique goals.
  • A Systemic Perspective: We look at how your environment, family history, societal expectations, and relational patterns influence how you feel and navigate the world.
  • Inclusive & Affirming Care: We are committed to honors-based, inclusive care that respects diverse backgrounds, identities, and lived experiences.

Who We Serve

Rooted Mental Health offers therapy for adults, couples, and families navigating:

  • Life transitions, identity shifts, and personal growth
  • Anxiety, stress, and burnout
  • Relational dynamics, communication challenges, and boundary-setting
  • Cultural identity, intergenerational dynamics, and systemic stressors
